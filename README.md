# Engineering learning catalogue (draft proof of concept)

> **This is a draft proof of concept.** It is a work in progress, is not official guidance and has not been reviewed. Topics, descriptions and learning links may be incomplete or wrong, and may change or be removed without notice.

View the catalogue: https://gds-dtx.github.io/engineering-learning-catalogue/

## What it is

A catalogue of what engineers need to know to build and run public sector software, organised by the stages of the software development life cycle. Each topic has a short description, the closest skill in the [Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/), and links to learning that may help.

## How it was made

- **Topics** come from analysis of public code in the [cross-government open source repository list](https://www.uk-x-gov-software-community.org.uk/xgov-opensource-repo-scraper/) (12 November 2026), the [GOV.UK developer documentation](https://docs.publishing.service.gov.uk/) and the [GDaD Capability Framework](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/).
- **Learning** comes from a snapshot of learning available to public sector engineers, taken in November 2026. Items were matched to topics by keyword or by hand, so check each item before relying on it.
- **Stages** are based on common phases of the [software development life cycle](https://fortranhouse.com/software-development-life-cycle-sdlc/).

## Where the learning comes from

Most of the learning comes from these platforms. Check each one for who can use it and any booking deadlines.

- [Digital and Data Hub: industry-led learning](https://governmentdigitalanddatahub.campaign.gov.uk/learn/digital-and-data-industry-led-learning/): free self-paced courses from technology providers. Open to anyone.
- [Get Tech Certified 2026](https://governmentdigitalanddatahub.campaign.gov.uk/learn/get-tech-certified/): free certifications and courses for public sector employees with a work email. Closes 31 December 2026.
- [Government Campus: Technology and Software](https://prospectus.governmentcampus.co.uk/04-technology-and-software/): trainer-led courses for civil servants, booked through the Learning Frameworks portal. Book by 31 January 2027.
- [Government Campus: Government Digital and Data Profession](https://prospectus.governmentcampus.co.uk/04-professions/government-digital-and-data-profession/): courses for the digital and data profession, grouped by level.
- [Civil Service Learning](https://learn.civilservice.gov.uk/): learning for all civil servants. Not yet reviewed for this catalogue.
- [National Careers Service: Skills Bootcamps](https://nationalcareers.service.gov.uk/find-a-course/filterresults?SideBar.CourseType.SelectedIds=Skills+Bootcamp&SideBar.Sectors.SelectedIds=7): digital bootcamps of up to 16 weeks for adults in England.
- [AI Skills Hub](https://aiskillshub.org.uk/): AI courses and programmes. Needs a free account.
- [Digital and Data Hub: Spotlight webinars](https://governmentdigitalanddatahub.campaign.gov.uk/learn/spotlight-webinars/): short introductory webinars, with past recordings.

## Frameworks and standards

These describe the skills, levels and standards the catalogue is built around.

- [Government Digital and Data Profession Capability Framework](https://understand-digital-data-roles-skills.service.gov.uk/): the roles, levels and skills used for assessment and career progression. Each topic is mapped to one of its skills.
- [Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual): guidance to help teams create and run public services that meet the Service Standard. The Technology section covers how to build, host, secure and maintain services.
- [The GDS Way](https://gds-way.digital.cabinet-office.gov.uk/): technology standards and good practice from the Government Digital Service.
- [GOV.UK Design System](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/): styles, components and patterns for building accessible services.
- [NCSC secure development and deployment guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/developers-collection): principles for developing and deploying software securely.
- [Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): the accessibility standard that services need to meet.

## Communities

- [Cross Government Software Engineering Community](https://www.uk-x-gov-software-community.org.uk/): events, conferences and special interest groups for engineers across the public sector.
- [Community events](https://uk-x-gov-software-community.github.io/events/): monthly lean coffees and GovDev conferences.
- [Special interest groups](https://uk-x-gov-software-community.github.io/special-interest/): including sustainable software, open source and Heads of Engineering.
- [Software developer learning pathways](https://github.com/uk-x-gov-software-community/software-developer-learning-pathways): a community-written pathway of developer competencies at junior, mid and senior level.

## Practise with real APIs

- [GOV.UK Content API](https://content-api.publishing.service.gov.uk/): no sign-up needed. A simple first API to practise with.
- [Companies House API](https://developer.company-information.service.gov.uk/): live company data with a free API key.
- [HMRC Developer Hub](https://developer.service.hmrc.gov.uk/api-documentation/docs/using-the-hub): a sandbox with test users for practising OAuth 2.0 and API integration.

## Technical details

The catalogue is a single HTML file (`index.html`) with no external scripts, fonts or tracking. It doesn't use cookies or store any data.
